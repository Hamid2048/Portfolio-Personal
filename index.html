About Me

Turning business processes into reliable software

I'm a Full Stack Developer who builds Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) systems and business applications with ASP.NET Core MVC and SQL Server. My day-to-day work covers the full path of a feature — from database design and stored procedures, through backend architecture and business logic, to interactive frontends with data grids, dashboards and reports.

I work in a layered architecture — Controller → Service → Repository — so business rules stay clean, testable and easy to extend. On the frontend I build responsive interfaces with JavaScript, jQuery, Bootstrap and DevExtreme grids; on the data side I write optimized SQL with stored procedures, CTEs and views that power financial and operational reporting.

Beyond core ERP modules, I integrate external systems through REST APIs — including FBR Digital Invoicing — and automate repetitive business workflows so teams spend less time on manual work and more time on decisions.
